Your guide to Space of Mind Leader

Train how you lead like you train your body.

Space of Mind Leader is a daily practice that rewires the thought patterns holding you back in the moments you lead, using your own voice and your own leadership goals.

Knowing how to lead isn't the same as doing it.

Close the gap with future-self training™

Prime

Morning · 5 minutes

Future You, in your own voice, guides you through a visualisation tailored to your day. Picture how you want to lead in the meetings ahead, so you start the day feeling more prepared.

Rewire

Belief work session · about 12 minutes

Explore one belief that shows up when you lead: where it came from, how it shapes your choices, and a more helpful belief to practise. Your growth path brings these beliefs together.

Embody

Rehearsal · about 10 minutes

Rehearse a real conversation with Future You before it happens. Pick a meeting from your calendar, design your own scenario or choose from one of the existing ones. Choose the pressure, and try out how you want to think, feel and respond. Then take that practice into the room.

Every week

This week's training

Short teaching audios on one move for one of your focus skills, with a transcript. Try it now in a rep, or keep a try-it line for a real meeting and say later whether you tried it. A new part opens each week; replay any of them from your behaviour ladder.

Sunday evening to Monday noon

Plan your week

“Choose the moments you want to practise.” Tick the meetings that matter, give each a practice day and time, and get a reminder then.

Growth paths

Two focus skills. One path of your own.

Each path is a sentence about who you're becoming, linked to the belief that gets in the way. Your two focus skills become your two paths; you can add one personal path just for you, which never appears in skill reporting.

For each path, pick a quality (Calm under pressure, Decisive, Direct, Steady, Open to being wrong, Warm and firm, Patient, Light) and say what someone would see you do. Embody counts the turns where you matched it.

Real conversations

Rehearse the meetings that matter.

The goal for your pilot: five real conversations, rehearsed and had.

01Choose

“Choose a conversation that matters.” Pick it from your calendar or add one by hand: who, the focus, what might be hard.

02Get ready

Rate how ready you feel, 1 to 5. Pick the pressure: Gentle, Direct or Intense.

03Rehearse

It plays like a phone call with the other person. Future You can whisper up to three hints, in your voice. Redo a line, or finish when you're ready.

04Carry it in

Rate your readiness again and set one line to see ten minutes before. Afterwards, check in: did it happen, and how did it compare with what you expected?

Your baseline rep

What changes

You become the leader you set out to be.

Not someday. In the next meeting, the next decision, the next hard conversation.

You lead from who you're becoming, not from old habits

You see the beliefs that used to make the call for you. “I need to have the answer.” “If I push back, I'll lose them.” Once you can name them, they stop deciding how you lead.

You stay steady when it counts

Your patterns have a schedule. Knowing when they tend to show up means you walk into the pressured moment ready, instead of reacting to it.

You've already had the hard conversation once

The feedback you kept postponing, the push-back you used to swallow, the room you went quiet in. You've rehearsed it with Future You, so when it's real it feels familiar, not frightening.

You hear yourself becoming that leader

Your voice shows it first: a steadier pace, fewer moments of strain. You can see when the old pattern spoke and when Future You did, in your own words.

You have proof of who you've become

Every time you spoke up, held a line or acted before you felt ready is logged. Evidence of the leader you're becoming that you can't argue with.
